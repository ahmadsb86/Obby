# 3/2 Reqs

To apply to the Caltech 3/2 program, applicants must have completed the following requirements at Haverford:
- One year of physics (but two years are recommended).
- One year of mathematics (including multivariable calculus and differential equations; again, two years are recommended).
- One year of chemistry.
